4. Evaluation report

Generated 23 Oct 2026. Numbers below are produced by the pipeline, not typed in.

1. What was built

A pipeline that (1) scrapes a US macro calendar and recent macro news, (2) generates trade logs for 36 parameter permutations of a moving-average strategy on SPY, (3) builds weekly time-based, event, news and market features, (4) trains a model to predict each permutation's next-week P&L, and (5) selects the predicted-best permutation each week under walk-forward validation.

2. Data

  • Events: Apify actor solidcode/economic-calendar-data-scraper (Investing.com calendar), filtered to united states, importance high. 36 events, 13 Apr 2026 to 18 Jun 2026. Times stored in UTC and US Eastern.
  • News: Tavily search, topic news, 5 queries (Federal Reserve interest rate decision; US CPI inflation report; US jobs report nonfarm payrolls; US economy outlook recession; US Treasury yields dollar), 14-day windows. 462 unique articles, 12 Apr 2026 to 09 Oct 2026.
  • Prices and trade logs: no trade logs were provided, so they were generated by backtesting on SPY 1h bars (854 bars, 13 Apr to 08 Oct 2026), giving 872 trades across 36 permutations (fast MA, slow MA, stop-loss, take-profit; 1 bp cost per side).
  • Sentiment: FinBERT (ProsusAI/finbert), score = P(positive) - P(negative).

3. Method

  • Target: total net return of the trades a permutation entered in a week (0 if it stayed flat).
  • Features (known at the start of the week): days since/until the next CPI, FOMC decision and jobs report; number of high-impact events scheduled that week; sign of the last CPI/jobs surprise and a 4-week surprise index; news sentiment over 7 and 28 days, its change, and article count; prior-week SPY return, volatility and range; each permutation's recent P&L and win rate; the permutation parameters.
  • Model: gradient boosting (scikit-learn HistGradientBoostingRegressor: depth 3, learning rate 0.05, 120 iterations, min leaf 20, L2 1.0), one model on all permutation-weeks. No tuning on test data.
  • Walk-forward: expanding window, one test week at a time, at least 4 training weeks. A training row is used only if all its trades had exited before the test week began (purging), because trades overlap weeks.
  • Selection and scoring: each test week the model picks the permutation with the highest predicted P&L, and the score is what that permutation actually earned. Metrics use weekly returns, annualised with sqrt(52); max drawdown is taken on the compounded equity curve.

4. Look-ahead controls

  • Strategy signals use the previous bar's close and trade at the next bar's open; if one bar touches both stop and target the stop is assumed to hit first; gaps fill at the open; costs are charged both ways.
  • Features use only information dated before the week starts, except the event schedule (what is due, days to the next release), which is published in advance. Actual release values are never used for the week they belong to.
  • Permutation history uses only trades that have already exited.

6. Limitations

  • Short sample. Weekly walk-forward over about 20 test weeks. The 36 permutations trade the same prices, so the effective sample is far smaller than the row count.
  • Self-generated trade logs from one simple strategy on one instrument. Results say nothing about the company's own strategies; the pipeline accepts a real trade log with the same columns.
  • Event coverage. Calendar events end 18 Jun 2026, earlier than prices and news (08 Oct 2026). The event-feature run is limited to the covered weeks, and the second run excludes events so it can use the full period.
  • No Nonfarm Payrolls events are in the event data, so the jobs-report features are empty. The calendar scrape was cut off by a row cap and needs re-running with the category filter.
  • Sentiment is approximate, and the model's use of it is only as good as the scorer.
  • Two feature sets were evaluated (with and without calendar events); no other variants were tried.
